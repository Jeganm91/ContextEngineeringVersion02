# Office Printer Setup Guide

This guide explains how to connect to shared office printers.

1. Open Settings > Printers on your laptop.
2. Select "Add Printer" and choose the printer nearest your desk from the
   list (e.g., "3F-East-HP-Color").
3. Enter your employee network credentials when prompted.
4. Print a test page to confirm the connection.

For toner or paper jam issues, submit a facilities ticket rather than a
software or IT access ticket.
